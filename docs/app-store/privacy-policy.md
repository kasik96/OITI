# Privacy Policy — Open Infrared Thermal Imager

_Last updated: 2026-11-25_

Open Infrared Thermal Imager ("the app") is a utility that displays the live image and
temperature data from a USB‑C thermal camera connected to your device.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

- The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party SDKs.
- It makes no network connections.
- The camera feed and temperature data are processed entirely on your device and are never
  uploaded anywhere.

## Photos and files you save
When you tap **Save**, the app writes a PNG image and a CSV of temperatures to the app's own
Documents folder on your device, which you can access through the Files app. These files stay on
your device and under your control; the app never sends them anywhere.

## Camera access
The app requests camera access solely to read frames from the connected USB thermal camera. It is
not used for any other purpose.
